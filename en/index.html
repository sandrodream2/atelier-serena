01 — The Philosophy

Un'idea di bellezza
che nasce dal benessere

Our thinking

Listening before
acting

Atelier Serena was born from a simple thought: the skin is a story to be read with delicacy. There are no identical formulas for everyone, because each person carries their own rhythm, sensitivity and moment in life.

That is why we work with personalised protocols, products selected for their purity and hands trained to listen before acting.

Serena Vitale Founder & Technical Director

01.1 — The Story

Fifteen years of unhurried gestures

In 2009 Serena Vitale opened a small studio in a Milanese courtyard, with a single cabin and the idea of offering treatments without haste. Over the years the atelier has grown, but the method remains the same: relaxed times, one client at a time, no overlapping appointments.

Today Atelier Serena occupies three rooms in Via della Spiga and welcomes a limited number of people each day. A deliberate choice, allowing us to dedicate to each one the time they deserve.

01.2 — The Values
I

Respect

No protocol is imposed. Every journey begins with a conversation and adapts to the skin, timing and expectations of the person in front of us.

II

We know and declare every ingredient we use. We select traceable formulations, free of aggressive substances and rigorously tested.

III

03.1 — The Method

Four steps,
no shortcuts

Phase 01

Consultation

Thirty minutes of listening and skin analysis, without obligation. Together we define realistic goals and the number of sessions needed.

Phase 02

Protocol

We build a personalised plan, with products and techniques chosen according to your skin’s response.

Treatment

Each session lasts as long as necessary, with no overlaps. We work in a silent environment, with calibrated light and temperature.

Maintenance

At the end of the cycle you receive daily guidance and a schedule of touch-ups to preserve results over time.

06 — Frequently Asked Questions

Before calling us

How does the first visit work?

The first consultation lasts about thirty minutes and is free and without obligation. Together we analyse the skin, habits and expectations, and define a realistic journey. Only after your consent do we proceed with the first treatment.

Should I arrive early for my appointment?

We recommend arriving five to ten minutes early so we can welcome you calmly, seat you in the reception and offer you a tea or infusion. There is no crowded waiting room: every appointment has no overlaps, so your time starts punctually.

How often should treatments be repeated?

It depends on the treatment: face journeys are most effective in cycles of 4-6 sessions weekly or bi-weekly, body treatments in cycles of four sessions, while a massage can be a single appointment or a monthly maintenance ritual.

Are the products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. We select traceable formulations, free of aggressive substances and rigorously tested. For particularly reactive skin we use specific lines and always perform a tolerability test before the treatment.

How can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Just call or write to us at least 24 hours in advance: we will reschedule the appointment at no cost. We kindly ask you to respect this deadline, because every time slot is dedicated to a single person.

Is it possible to gift a treatment?

Certainly. You can gift a single treatment or an open-value voucher, valid for twelve months. Contact us and we will prepare a handwritten card, ready to give.
